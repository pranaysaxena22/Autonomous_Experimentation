# Sealed acceptance lab: ledger (append-only)

Every creation, sealing, run or opening of the sealed lab is recorded here, newest last. Entries start with `- [date] TYPE`. Types: `CREATED`, `SEALED`, `ACCEPTANCE RUN`, `OPENED` (breaks the seal).
Nothing above a line is ever edited.

- [2026-10-11] CREATED: `sealed_lab.py` written, version 1. Pre-seal mechanics tests only, by a throwaway script outside the project that loaded the file by path: determinism and order independence, direction of each effect, pass shares of 40,000 random recipes,
  noise sizes, and the shape of a generated history. No model and no decision engine was involved. The properties were fixed before the first test. The first test missed two of them (random recipes passing all three targets: 24.3%, wanted 3% to 12%;
  date drift in the history: 0.11, wanted at least 0.25), so shape constants were adjusted twice: (1) the dissolution time scale 15 to 19 and its porosity sensitivity 5 to 8, the friability lamination threshold 0.085 to 0.095, and the history's supplier mix, centre spread (0.08 to 0.06), exploratory share (35% to 25%) and
  centre-time width (0.25 to 0.15); (2) the dissolution time scale 19 to 22 and monotone drug and binder in the history's four campaign centres. After the second round every property held (all three targets together 9.0%, dissolution 22%, hardness 41.6%, friability 80%; largest date drift 0.55; supplier explains up to 18.5% of an input's spread).
- [2026-10-11] SEALED: fingerprints recorded in `SEAL.json` and `docs/decisions.md`; git tag `sealed-lab-v1`. No run since the properties above were checked. From this entry on, any run other than a designated acceptance run, and any change, is a violation.
- [2026-10-12] VIOLATION: two of the 40 acceptance seeds were used before the first seal, so a choice was made after looking at results that included them (rule 4 of the README). Seed 7,000,001 generated the 3,000-experiment history (twice per run) and 2,000 measurements at one recipe whose aggregate statistics (date drift, supplier share of the recipes' spread, replicates per experiment, noise sizes) informed the two shape-constant adjustments recorded in the CREATED entry; seed 7,000,002 produced one measurement (to check that a different seed gives different noise). The same two seeds were used in all three runs of the throwaway test script (the first test and the two adjustment rounds); no other acceptance seed was used. They were deliberate, convenient choices (the first two numbers of the range), not a loop over the range and not an accident of a wider pool; the first README and the CREATED entry did not name them. The pass-share statistics (24.3%, 13.1%, 9.0%) did not depend on any acceptance seed (40,000 random recipes from an unrelated generator, noise-free truth with no batch effect). Recorded by the project owner's instruction as a violation under the rule as written.
- [2026-10-12] RESEALED: version 2, tag `sealed-lab-v2`. The acceptance seeds were moved from 7,000,001-7,000,040 to 7,000,101-7,000,140, a block never generated, run or examined in any form (checked against every project file, every scratch script and the whole conversation). Nothing else in the lab changed: `sealed_lab.py` differs from version 1 in that one line only (git shows 1 insertion and 1 deletion); every formula, constant, the noise, the history generator and the targets are byte for byte as they were. 7,000,001-7,000,040 are RETIRED and may be kept only as disclosed, already-seen development reference points. New fingerprints in `SEAL.json` and `docs/decisions.md`. Two of the original 40 seeds were examined before the first seal, so the range was moved to restore a fully blind acceptance seed set. No run since.
